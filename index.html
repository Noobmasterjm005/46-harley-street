FAQ

Am I a candidate?

That is what the consultation is for. Dr James looks at your teeth, your health, and what you want before anyone talks about treatment.

How long is the appointment?

The implant consultation is 30 minutes and free. The full clinical assessment is a separate visit.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on your case. You leave the consultation with a time frame for the whole implant plan, which is separate from the length of one visit.

What happens at the consultation?

You meet Dr James, talk through your smile goals and concerns, and hear whether implants may be suitable. The scan and written plan are part of the full clinical assessment, not the free visit.

Our expertise

Few dentists are qualified in sedation dentistry... even fewer in polypharmacy sedation.

Dr James is a highly qualified sedation dentist in London. Although, his skills go beyond your standard sedation dentistry. His postgraduate diploma in sedation dentistry means he is qualified in polypharmacy sedation dentistry. This means he can deliver several different IV medications safely. Dr James’ expertise in the area has resulted in his accreditation by RCS England to teach and train others in sedation services.

Dr Anthony James

Specialty Doctor and Lead Dentist at The Harley Street Dental Implant Centre.

BDS (Lond) MFDS RCS (Eng) MJDF DipImpDent (Manc) DipDSed (KCL) AKC PGCertClinEd (KCL) FHEA MAcadMEd

Dr. James qualified in 2011 from King’s College London. Subsequently, he worked as a Junior Doctor in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, progressing to the role of Specialty Doctor in the field. During this time he held the position of Clinical Lecturer in Oral Surgery at The University of Bristol.

He has since completed a postgraduate diploma in Dental Implantology and provides a comprehensive scope of Implantology practice, having now placed over 4000 successful implants. His deep experience of dental implants has led to his accredited role as a Mentor in Dental Implants by the College of General Dentistry.

Accordingly, Dr. James’ experience has led to him being selected to be an Examiner for The Faculty of Dental Surgery at The Royal College of Surgeons of England.

Dr Maria Sobierajska

Specialist Prosthodontist

BDS (Distinction) (Lond), AKC, Pg cert, MJDF RCS Eng, M Prost RCSEd, MClinDent Prosthodontics (Distinction) (Lond)

Maria Sobierajska is a specialist dentist with a strong academic and professional background. She graduated with honors from King’s College London in 2015, where she earned her bachelor’s degree in dentistry and became an associate of the college.

Following her graduation, Maria spent a year working in the oral and maxillofacial department, where she gained valuable experience in complex extractions, facial trauma, and dental emergencies. Since then, she has worked in various dental practices across London and East Sussex, continually expanding her expertise. She has also pursued several advanced qualifications, including the MJDF from the Royal College of Surgeons of England, a course in Aesthetic Restorative Procedures from the Eastman Dental Institute, a Primary Dental Care Certification from the University of Kent and a National Course in Conscious Sedation from the Society for the Advancement of Anaesthesia in Dentistry.

Maria developed a keen interest in rehabilitating patients with complex dental conditions such as severe tooth wear, hypodontia, and tooth loss. This passion led her to pursue specialist training in prosthodontics at King’s College London, which she completed with honors in September 2023. She also successfully passed her prosthodontic specialty exams with the Royal College of Surgeons in Edinburgh.

Known for her commitment to delivering high-quality, evidence-based, and ethical care, Maria is deeply appreciated by her patients for her compassionate nature and positive demeanor.

Languages spoken: English (fluent), Polish (fluent) and French (basic).

Restorative Dentist
