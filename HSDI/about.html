Our clinicians

The people you will see

Dr James leads implant and oral surgery care. Dr Maria Sobierajska plans smile work. Dr Sherif Abbas Ali provides restorative dentistry and sedation.

Dr Anthony James

Specialty doctor and lead clinician

Qualified in 2011 from King’s College London, then oral and maxillofacial surgery and a lectureship at the University of Bristol. He has placed over 4,000 implants, mentors other dentists, and is an examiner for the Royal College of Surgeons of England. He also provides IV sedation.

Dr Maria Sobierajska

Specialist prosthodontist

Graduated from King’s College London in 2015 and completed specialist prosthodontic training there in 2023. She plans smile makeovers, tooth wear, veneers, and tooth replacement. She speaks English, Polish, and basic French.

Dr Sherif Abbas Ali

Restorative dentist

Trained in restorative dentistry at the Eastman Dental Institute. He combines clear aligners with bonding and veneers, and also provides oral surgery and sedation. He speaks Arabic.

The clinic also offers Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry, and general care, including emergencies, hygiene, and fillings.

Our dentists are GDC registered. Implants are Nobel Biocare and Straumann.
