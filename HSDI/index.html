Questions before you book

Something else on your mind? Write to info@harleystreetimplantcentre.co.uk.

What happens at the first visit?

You meet Dr Anthony James at 46 Harley Street. He listens to what you want from your teeth, examines your mouth, and explains which options may suit you. You leave with a clear next step, and time to decide.

Will treatment be painful?

Implant treatment is carried out with local anaesthetic, so the procedure itself should not hurt. Afterwards, most people manage with ordinary pain relief and the written aftercare from the clinic. You are told what to expect before anything is booked.

How much do implants cost?

There is no single fee. The estimate depends on your mouth and how many teeth are being replaced. At the first visit you are given a written estimate you can understand before treatment starts.

How long does treatment take?

A typical implant needs several months from the first visit to the final tooth, because the implant has to settle. Some appointments are short. Dr James sets out a timetable for your own case, rather than a generic schedule.

Who is a suitable candidate?

Many adults with one missing tooth, or several, can be assessed for implants. Gum health, the bone that supports an implant, and your general health all matter. Suitability is decided at the consultation, not from a form online.

What happens after treatment?

You receive written aftercare and a plan for review visits. The clinic explains how to look after the implant at home. If a concern arises, you know who to call at 46 Harley Street and what the next step will be.
