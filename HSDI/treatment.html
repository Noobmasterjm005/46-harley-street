Treatments

Care for a single tooth, a full arch, or a same-day problem.

Implants and surgery

Dental implants

A titanium root for a crown, bridge, or denture. Over 4,000 placed, with a 99.7% success rate.

Learn more about dental implants →

All-on-4

Four to six implants hold a fixed full arch, instead of a denture that comes out.

Learn more about All-on-4 →

Zygomatic implants

For severe bone loss in the upper jaw, anchored in the cheekbone.

Learn more about zygomatic implants →

Bone graft

A block of your own bone rebuilds the jaw before an implant is placed.

Learn more about bone grafts →

Guided bone regeneration

Graft material and a membrane help new bone form around an implant.

Learn more about guided bone regeneration →

Sinus lift

Adds bone beneath the upper sinus so an implant has enough height.

Learn more about sinus lifts →

Wisdom tooth extraction

A fully erupted wisdom tooth can often be removed without a surgical flap.

Learn more about wisdom tooth extraction →

Frenectomy

Releases a tight band under the tongue or inside the lip. Adults only.

Learn more about frenectomy →

Invisalign

Composite bonding

Tooth-coloured resin for a chip, a small gap, or an uneven edge, often in one visit.

Learn more about composite bonding →

Veneers

A thin porcelain facing on the front of a tooth. Usually two or three visits.

Learn more about veneers →

Crowns and bridges
