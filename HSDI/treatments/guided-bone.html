Guided bone regeneration

Guided bone regeneration encourages new bone where an implant would otherwise lack support. A graft material and a barrier membrane keep soft tissue out while bone fills in.

Book a consultation

Starting priceFrom £400
TimelineMonths
AnaesthesiaLocal anaesthetic. Sedation is available.

What the treatment involves

  1. Access The gum is opened and the implant site is prepared.
  2. Graft Bone graft material is placed as a scaffold.
  3. Membrane A barrier membrane covers the graft.
  4. Healing Integration takes months and is reviewed in clinic.

Who it may suit

  • Often done at the same time as implant placement.
  • Results take months, depending on the size of the graft and how you heal.
  • Local anaesthetic is used. Sedation is available.
