All-on-4 dental implants

All-on-4 uses four to six implants to hold a fixed full arch of teeth, for the upper jaw, the lower jaw, or both.

It is a fixed alternative to a denture that comes out.

Book a consultation

Starting priceFrom £13,500
AnaesthesiaLocal anaesthetic. Sedation is available.

What the treatment involves

  1. Planning The plan sets where four to six implants will sit and how the arch will look.
  2. Placement Implants are placed in denser areas of bone. Extra grafting is often avoided.
  3. Attachment A custom arch of teeth is fixed to the implants.
  4. Adjustments The fit and the bite are checked before you leave.

Who it may suit

  • Fewer implants than a tooth-by-tooth full mouth.
  • A stable bite, so eating is less limited than with a loose denture.
  • Day-to-day care is brushing, flossing, and reviews.
  • Local anaesthetic is used. Sedation is available if you are anxious.
