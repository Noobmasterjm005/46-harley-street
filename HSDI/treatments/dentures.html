Dentures

Premium dentures replace missing teeth and can be taken out. They may replace a few teeth or a full arch.

Implant-supported dentures clip or fix to implants for a more secure fit.

Book a consultation

Starting priceFrom £500
TimelineMaking can take a few weeks

What the treatment involves

  1. Consultation The mouth is assessed, and scans or impressions are taken.
  2. Design A custom shape is planned. Implant support is discussed if the bone allows it.
  3. Making The laboratory makes the denture in high-quality materials. This can take a few weeks.
  4. Fitting The fit is checked in the clinic and adjusted.

Who it may suit

  • Clean them daily with a denture brush, not ordinary toothpaste.
  • Soak them overnight.
  • Take them out at night.
  • With care they often last 5 to 10 years, then need review as the mouth changes.
