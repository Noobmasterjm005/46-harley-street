Smile makeover

A smile makeover is a personal plan that may combine whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns, or aligners.

Plans are led by Dr Maria Sobierajska, who qualified with honours from King’s College London in 2015 and has trained in aesthetic restorative dentistry.

Book a consultation

AppointmentsOne visit or several

What the treatment involves

  1. Consultation Goals and dental health are assessed. X-rays or 3D imaging can be done on site.
  2. Plan The combination of treatments is agreed before anything starts.
  3. Treatment Care may take one visit or several, depending on the plan.
  4. Review A follow-up checks that the result is holding.

Who it may suit

  • The fee depends on which treatments are included and is confirmed in the plan.
  • Finance options are discussed before you commit.
