Composite bonding

Composite bonding adds tooth-coloured resin to repair a chip, a small gap, or an uneven edge.

It often happens in one visit and removes little or no enamel.

Book a consultation

Starting price£350 per tooth
AppointmentsOften one visit

What the treatment involves

  1. Consultation The tooth and the result you want are checked.
  2. Preparation The surface is lightly roughened and a bonding liquid is applied.
  3. Application Resin matched to the tooth is layered and shaped.
  4. Finish A light hardens the resin, then it is polished.

Who it may suit

  • Avoid using bonded teeth to open packets or bite nails.
  • Coffee and strong colours can stain resin over time.
  • Use a non-abrasive toothpaste.
