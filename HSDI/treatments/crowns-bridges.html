Crowns and bridges

A crown is a cap that rebuilds a worn, broken, or heavily filled tooth.

A bridge replaces a missing tooth by anchoring an artificial tooth to the teeth, or implants, on either side.

What the treatment involves

  1. Assessment The tooth, or the gap, is examined with digital impressions or 3D imaging when needed.
  2. Preparation The tooth is shaped, or the supporting teeth are prepared.
  3. Laboratory The crown or bridge is made to the agreed colour and fit.
  4. Fit It is cemented and the bite is checked.

Who it may suit

  • A crown restores a damaged tooth. It does not replace a missing one.
  • A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth.
  • An implant is the alternative when you do not want neighbouring teeth prepared.
