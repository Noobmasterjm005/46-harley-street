Dental bone graft

A bone graft rebuilds jawbone that has shrunk, so an implant has enough bone to hold.

A block graft uses bone from your own jaw, often near the lower wisdom teeth or the chin.

Book a consultation

Starting priceFrom £1,500
AnaesthesiaLocal anaesthetic. Sedation is available.

What the treatment involves

  1. Harvest A block of bone is taken and shaped to the gap.
  2. Placement The block is fixed into the area that needs volume.
  3. Healing The graft is left to join the existing bone. This is checked over time.
  4. Implant The implant is placed once the new bone is ready.

Who it may suit

  • It can rebuild a larger defect than a small graft.
  • The new bone gives the implant a firmer base.
  • The procedure is done under local anaesthetic. Sedation is available.
