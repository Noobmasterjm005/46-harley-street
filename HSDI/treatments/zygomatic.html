Zygomatic implants

Zygomatic implants are for severe bone loss in the upper jaw. They anchor in the cheekbone rather than in jawbone that is too thin for a conventional implant.

Book a consultation

AnaesthesiaUsually local anaesthetic

What the treatment involves

  1. Imaging A CBCT scan maps the bone before a personalised plan is made.
  2. Placement Implants are placed into the zygomatic bone, usually under local anaesthetic.
  3. Temporary teeth A temporary restoration can sometimes be fitted at the same visit.
  4. Final teeth Crowns, a bridge, or a full arch are attached once the implants have integrated.

Who it may suit

  • Useful when the upper jaw has too little bone for standard implants.
  • Often avoids a separate bone graft.
  • Expect some swelling and a soft diet for a few days afterwards.
