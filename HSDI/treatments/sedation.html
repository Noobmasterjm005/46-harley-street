Sedation dentistry

Conscious sedation keeps you relaxed and drowsy while you stay able to respond. It is not a general anaesthetic.

Dr James holds a postgraduate diploma in sedation and is accredited to teach sedation. IV sedation can use more than one medicine when that is appropriate.

Book a consultation

Starting priceFrom £350 per hour

What the treatment involves

  1. Assessment Anxiety, medical history, and the treatment decide which form of sedation fits.
  2. IV sedation Medicine is given through a cannula. Heart rate, breathing, and blood pressure are monitored.
  3. Other options Inhalation sedation and oral sedation are lighter and suit shorter or milder anxiety.
  4. Afterwards You need an escort home after IV sedation, and you should not drive.

Who it may suit

  • IV sedation is for people with stronger dental anxiety or a longer procedure.
  • Most people are drowsy and remember little of the treatment.
  • The fee is from £350 per hour.
