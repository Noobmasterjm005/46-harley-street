Dental implants

Implants are titanium screws placed in the jaw to support a crown, bridge, or denture.

Dr James has placed over 4,000 implants, with a success rate of 99.7 percent. A CBCT scan is included in the implant consultation.

What the treatment involves

  1. Consultation Your mouth, goals, and whether an implant is suitable are discussed.
  2. Planning The number of implants, the restoration, and a 3D radiograph are set out.
  3. Placement The titanium implant is placed and left to fuse with the bone.
  4. Restoration A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached once integration is complete.

Who it may suit

  • Missing teeth can lead to bone loss. An implant helps keep the bone stimulated.
  • Hard or sticky foods are best avoided while a new implant settles.
  • Reviews and cleaning protect the implant after treatment.
